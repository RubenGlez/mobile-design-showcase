The method

What changes when the design is treated as a product decision.

  1. 01
    Read the context

    Audience, environment, platform, constraints, task success.

  2. 02
    Design the states

    Not just the default screen, also loading, denied, offline, error, and recovery.

  3. 03
    Verify the contract

    Accessibility, window size, input methods, localization, and real runtime limits.
